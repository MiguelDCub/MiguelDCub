# Hi, I'm Miguel Fernández 👋

### PhD Student in Medical Physics | Nuclear Physicist | Scientific Software & Instrumentation

I'm a **PhD student in Medical Physics at the University of Turin (UniTO), Italy**, with a background in Nuclear Physics and research experience at **INFN Torino**.

My academic and technical interests lie at the intersection of **physics, electronics, and software engineering**, particularly in particle detector instrumentation, high-precision timing systems, scientific computing, and automated data acquisition.

I enjoy developing software and experimental tools to solve complex scientific and engineering problems, combining physical principles with practical programming and instrumentation.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Medical Physics:** Charged Particle Therapy (CPT), Prompt Gamma Timing (PGT), and real-time treatment monitoring.
- **Particle Detectors:** Silicon detectors, Ultra-Fast Silicon Detectors (UFSD), and fast readout electronics.
- **Timing & Electronics:** High-precision timing measurements, FPGA-based Time-to-Digital Converters (TDCs), and ASIC characterization.
- **Data Acquisition:** Hardware-software integration, automated measurements, and experimental data acquisition systems.
- **Signal Processing:** Waveform analysis, timing resolution, jitter characterization, and statistical analysis of experimental data.
